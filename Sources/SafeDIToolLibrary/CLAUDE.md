# SafeDIToolLibrary

The `scan`, `generate`, `query` and `dump` subcommands; the `SafeDITool` executable (`Sources/SafeDITool`) only runs their root command. Rules: `.claude/rules/serialization.md` (the scan cache and `.safedi` files), `.claude/rules/query-needs.md`, `.claude/rules/file-paths.md`, `.claude/rules/module-membership-and-access.md` and `.claude/rules/conditional-compilation.md`.
